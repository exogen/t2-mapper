Film simulation LUTs

Fuji Velvia 50, Kodak Portra 160 NC, and Ilford HP5 Plus 400 are film
emulation presets by Pat David, distributed in Natron's HaldCLUT collection
under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license.
https://github.com/NatronGitHub/clut
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

MapGenius resampled the original sRGB HaldCLUT images to 32×32×32,
8-bit RGB LUT strips. The adapted PNGs in luts/film are distributed under
the same license. The film stock names identify the looks being approximated.
